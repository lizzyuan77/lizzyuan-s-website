Research

Undergraduate Thesis - NLP and Financial Text Analysis

Explored investor questions and corporate responses through financial text analysis, with a focus on topic modeling and information demand across platforms.

Insight: This project taught me that market behavior is often reflected not only in prices, but also in the language investors use to ask for information.

Intelligent Course Advisory AI Agent

Designed a personalized Dify recommendation module using multi-path workflows and a course-review knowledge base.

Insight: Building an advisory agent showed me how structured data, user intent, and final-answer design shape the usefulness of AI tools.

Commercial Bank Analysis

Conducted comparative analysis of different types of Chinese banks through annual reports and financial indicators, focusing on capital, liquidity, and strategic positioning.

Insight: Comparing banks showed me that regulation, business model, and balance-sheet structure shape strategy together rather than separately.

Corporate Finance Research Assistant

Worked as a research assistant on a corporate finance project, supporting data collection, cleaning, and preliminary empirical analysis.

Insight: This experience helped me see that financing decisions often reflect strategic intent and institutional context, not just textbook theory.

Predictive Modeling for Social Media Engagement

Built regression-based models to study how post characteristics influence engagement outcomes on social platforms.

Insight: I learned that real-world behavioral data is often non-linear, which makes model choice and interpretation just as important as prediction itself.

China’s Pharmaceutical Industry Chain Analysis

Mapped and analyzed the pharmaceutical industry chain using market and company-level data.

Insight: This project showed me how sector research depends on connecting scattered firm-level information to broader structural trends.
